AI + Messaging

WhatsApp "Catch Me Up"

AI-Powered Group Chat Digest — PRD & Product Story

"From 30 minutes of scrolling to 30 seconds of clarity."

The Problem

2.8B WhatsApp users, 700M+ active groups, yet no way to separate signal from noise. Users either drown in notifications or mute groups and miss critical info — a school closure, a work decision, a family emergency.

Key Insight

The only tool users had was muting — like unplugging the fire alarm because it goes off too often. It solves the noise but creates a new problem: you stop hearing the real fires.

The Solution

An on-demand, on-device AI digest that summarizes group chats into 4 categories: action items for you, decisions made, unanswered questions, and links/files shared. Entirely opt-in, privacy-first (zero messages leave device), generates in <2 seconds.

Impact & Process

▸

Validated across 6 independent research sources (Trustpilot, Reddit, clinical studies, competitive signals)

▸

RICE-scored P0 (8.6) against 9 competing problems

▸

Defined OKRs: 30% weekly active usage, <30s time-to-catch-up, 4.2+ CSAT

▸

Built wireframes, event tracking schema, edge cases, and 20-week roadmap

Trust & Learning

YouTube Learning Trust

3-Phase Product Analysis — Research → Prioritization → Solution

"The problem isn't content availability. It's confidence and structure."

The Problem

Frequent learners on YouTube struggle to identify trustworthy, learning-appropriate videos. 40%+ cite trust as their biggest frustration. 50%+ spend 5-15 minutes just finding the right video. The algorithm optimizes for engagement, not learning outcomes.

Key Insight

Users expend massive cognitive effort validating credibility and manually constructing learning paths. Over time, this erodes trust and reduces repeated reliance on YouTube for learning.

The Solution

Expert-Verified Creator Badge (ICE: 9.0) — domain-specific credential verification displayed on videos. Plus AI-Summarized Comment Consensus (ICE: 6.0) — structured audience feedback reducing comment noise into trust signals.

Impact & Process

▸

Conducted user interviews + surveys (80% daily learners, 40%+ trust issues)

▸

Built personas grounded in real behavior (Rishit: exam prep, Isha: professional upskilling)

▸

PIF-scored 6 problems; trust deficit scored 15/15

▸

Goal: improve 30-day retention by 15% for weekly learners in 6 months

Travel & Retention

Improving Google Maps

Strategic Product Roadmap — Retention-Focused Growth

"Solving fragmentation unlocks Google's unique ecosystem advantage."

The Problem

Travelers struggle with information scattered across multiple platforms — tickets in Gmail, reservations in apps, IDs in local storage. No single source of truth during a trip. Google Maps is stuck as a pure utility (A→B) when it could own the entire travel journey.

Key Insight

Travel involves high-ticket transactions and cross-surface synergy (Gmail + Calendar + Maps) that no competitor can replicate. Consolidating the fragmented journey creates deep ecosystem lock-in.

The Solution

Agentic Travel Assistant MVP (Impact: 5/5, Effort: 4/5) — proactive notifications, suggestions, and human-in-the-loop bookings. Built on Travel Vault (aggregating Gmail/Calendar data into centralized itinerary).

Impact & Process

▸

Segmented 4 user types; prioritized Travelers over Commuters for retention potential

▸

Identified 3 friction points; P1 (fragmentation) selected via usage data + user feedback

▸

Ranked 3 solutions on Impact vs. Effort matrix

▸

Defined KPIs: DAU retention, notification CTR, trips created, ad revenue

Prepd.ai

AI-Powered Meal Planning — Built, Shipped, Measured

"78% activation rate. 68% Day-7 retention. Zero CAC."

The Problem

73% of international students surveyed reported decision fatigue around daily cooking. The mental overhead of figuring out what to cook, with what ingredients, within budget constraints — it's a daily drain.

Key Insight

Decision fatigue around meals isn't about recipes — it's about the gap between what's in your fridge and what you could make. Bridging that gap with AI ingredient scanning (92% accuracy) removes the hardest step.

The Solution

AI-powered meal planning app with ingredient scanning and personalized recipe generation. Shipped MVP in 10 days. Instrumented with PostHog (15+ events). Ran A/B test (n=34) that pivoted roadmap to real-time generation.
