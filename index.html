  • Build vs buy

    Won funding to build the software in-house, cutting recurring vendor cost ~70% and keeping proprietary data inside.

    Service complaints, warranty data, field trials and global benchmarks showed fleets would pay a premium for lower running cost: about $1.2K a year in fuel savings against diesel.

04 · Method

Three habits from the garage.

Habit 1

Instrument before you argue.

Opinions are cheap when the funnel is logged. Find where people drop, then decide what to build.

At See.Me, 30–40 artist interviews came before a single line of the listing feature.

Habit 2

Evals are the spec.

An LLM feature is done when it passes tests you wrote before you liked the output.

Code and LLM-judge evals gated the See.Me listing feature. Spar.ai rejects coaching that is not grounded in the user's words.

Habit 3

Name the trade.

Every product decision costs something. Say what, out loud, with a number.

On the Electric Ace: about 10% of range, given up on purpose, for safety in extreme heat.
