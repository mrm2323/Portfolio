Product Strategy Case Study

Improving Google Maps

A strategic product roadmap focused on increasing active usage and traveler retention

Product Strategy Retention UX Case Study Agentic AI Go-to-Market

Google Maps has 1B+ MAUs, but it's stuck as a utility — people open it, navigate, and leave. I asked: what if Maps became your proactive travel companion?

I identified travelers as the highest-value underserved segment, defined the fragmented travel information problem through user research, and proposed an Agentic Travel Assistant — an AI layer that unifies Gmail confirmations, Calendar itineraries, and Maps location intelligence into one proactive experience. The strategy includes a 3-phase GTM rollout (100K → 1M → Global), a human-in-the-loop trust architecture, and a $15M investment case with clear success metrics.
